# It All Started with a Routine Checkup About 20 years ago, when I was 36, a routine health check revealed that I had high blood pressure. For a while, my condition was simply monitored. I think I took it somewhat lightly, assuming that a little extra care would be enough to keep things under control. # Being Told I Might Have an Adrenal Tumor A few years later, my blood pressure readings worsened as my physical condition changed, and I underwent more detailed examinations. The results revealed a suspected tumor in my adrenal gland. I was also told that it would not be possible to determine whether the tumor was benign or malignant until after surgery. Based on the examination results, I was informed that there was a high likelihood of malignancy. After surgery, the pathology results confirmed that the tumor was malignant. It was also around this time that the diagnosis of pheochromocytoma appears to have been confirmed.
