# Living Well While Monitoring a Benign Tumor

*There is no proven lifestyle approach that can reliably prevent a benign tumor from becoming malignant.*

Rather than making strenuous efforts to try to shrink the tumor, I believe it is more important to maintain my overall health and feel comfortable and at ease in my daily life.

Here are some of the things I try to keep in mind:

Get enough rest and avoid allowing fatigue to build up.

Avoid taking on too much stress and find personal ways to maintain emotional balance.

Pay attention to the basics of a healthy lifestyle, including a balanced diet and sufficient sleep.

When I notice changes in my physical condition, avoid pushing myself and consult my doctor when necessary.

Keep up with regular medical checkups to monitor the condition of the tumor.

In particular, when my physical condition or energy levels are limited, I try to follow one simple principle: If something can wait until tomorrow, there is no need to do it today.

I cannot determine the condition or behavior of a tumor on my own. That is precisely why I believe it is important to continue the necessary medical examinations in consultation with my doctor, while also taking good care of my body in everyday life.
