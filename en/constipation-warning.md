
# When Constipation Became My Warning Sign

When chronic fatigue builds up and my health begins to deteriorate, I’ve noticed that there are usually several early warning signs.

For me, one of the clearest is **a change in my bowel habits**.

I usually have a natural bowel movement within 30 minutes of waking up. But when fatigue starts to build, it gradually takes longer, or I may become constipated.

I used to pay little attention to this change. Looking back, though, I think my body was already telling me that it needed rest.

At this stage, I can still manage my daily activities. But from late afternoon into the evening, I begin to feel more fatigued, and my ability to concentrate and make decisions gradually declines.

If I keep pushing myself, I may fall asleep as soon as I get into bed, almost as if I’ve blacked out—quite unlike my usual sleep. But that sleep often lasts only an hour or two, after which I’m unable to fall asleep again.

The next day, intense fatigue and stiffness set in. I’ve experienced this pattern many times, and it has often left me unable to do much for about two days.

To avoid repeating this cycle, I now treat **irregular bowel movements as a yellow warning light** that my condition may be getting worse.

Rather than seeing constipation itself as the main problem, I’ve learned to take it as a sign that fatigue is building up and my ability to recover may be declining. This helps me rest sooner and adjust the pace of my daily life.

For me, bowel habits are more than a digestive issue. They have become an important barometer that gives me an objective clue about my overall condition.
